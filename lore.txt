WHY PINBALL?
	It should somehow build off of traditional high fantasy.
	These artifacts are ancient, from the days of adventuring and before. Decades ago, there was a man who was a world-record holder for his pinball skills. Unfortunately, he was also a mastermind in stealing stuff, so he would often steal these ancient artifacts from museums and individuals in planned heists, then would seal the artifacts in different pinball machines using magic so that only he was skilled enough to unlock them and retrieve the artifacts.
	One day he died in a failed heist; the pinball machines with the stolen artifacts have been returned to the rightful owners. One of the owners was the father of the company's CEO, some rich guy with a family history of being rich and sort of evil. The CEO was too lazy to get good at pinball himself, so he sourced a couple hundred 'employees' to work hard and unlock this pinball machine for him.
	You are one of these 'employees'. The hours are long and the pay is not good. You love pinball but regret having signed that contract. So, you do what you must: train hard at pinball and unlock the CEO's pinball machine. Not only would you become the best pinball player in the world, but you would win your agency back.
	Once you win, if your employment contract is still intact, you must surrender the artifact to the evil CEO. If you managed to loophole your way out, you can use the artifact to defeat the CEO.
